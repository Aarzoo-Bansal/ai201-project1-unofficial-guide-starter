I can give one more star for the smooth move-in process.
I have been living at Miro Apartments for almost a year due to work, but unfortunately, the overall experience has been far from satisfactory.
Despite being advertised as a luxury apartment, the smoking issue has never been addressed. Every day, I have to endure secondhand smoke coming through the ventilation system. To cope, I must constantly keep the exhaust fan and air purifier running, which also drives up my electricity bill. Has anyone really taken action? The answer is no—nobody seems to care.
A so-called “membership management fee” is automatically added to your rent from the second month and cannot be canceled. No one informs you about any activities, nor do they provide any instructions. The app reminders are useless, and in many cases you can’t even find where these activities are held. If no one is participating, why force this fee on everyone? Shouldn’t only those who want to join pay? I have lived in several “luxury” apartments in New York and Los Angeles, and none of them charged such meaningless and non-refundable fees. This is simply unacceptable.
There is a $25 package fee, yet packages are often delayed by 1–2 days before being delivered. While I understand checking items takes time, this delay creates serious inconvenience—especially when ordering perishable food.
Management frequently called me in the middle of the night, accusing me of making noise because “someone in high heels” was walking in my unit. The reality: my wife, my cat, and I were asleep in bed. So apparently someone was walking in high heels in my apartment without us knowing? Even after multiple explanations, these calls continued for nearly two weeks. I eventually lost patience and demanded they install a noise monitor in my unit. Only then did the calls stop. No apology was offered, and as far as I can tell, no corrective action was ever taken.
Just find somewhere else. You can pay the same rent elsewhere and get a much better apartment—without all these unnecessary headaches.
