# Source: The Ryden Apartments
# Address: 138 Balbach St, San Jose, CA 95110

First of all, we’d like to share how wonderful our stay was at The Ryden Apartments for nearly 1.5 years.
The leasing manager has been incredibly friendly, kind, and on top of everything—from our move-in process to tracking packages and addressing any concerns during our stay. We chose The Ryden because it’s located in the heart of San Jose, with easy access to grocery stores, the post office, restaurants, and more. The Ryden is also one of the quietest apartment communities we’ve lived in within the South Bay. The residents are kind, warm, and respectful, and it’s surprisingly easy to find parking within the community—even near downtown San Jose.
Whenever something needed fixing, the maintenance team was always quick and responsive. The maintenance technician is especially amazing—he knows everything about the building and always makes sure things are taken care of. The cleaning crew also does a fantastic job keeping the building spotless, and the elevators are always clean and fresh.
During our move-out process, the leasing manager was incredibly thoughtful and even reassured us that staying a few extra days would be completely fine if needed. It’s rare to find an apartment manager like this—someone who truly cares about residents and always greets you with a smile when you have questions or need help.
We highly recommend The Ryden as one of the best apartment communities in the area, especially if you’re new to the South Bay. If you’re looking for a peaceful, well-maintained, and welcoming place to live, this is absolutely worth checking out.
