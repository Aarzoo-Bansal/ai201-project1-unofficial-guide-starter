I live in the area basically in between these two complexes and all of it is safe, but semi-urban. As noted, there are unhoused people but they generally just want to be left alone and if someone is having a bad day you can avoid them. Nice wide sidewalks in this area.
I would disagree about Alameda being better for bus transit and downtown. Cahill Park is definitely superior for a person without a car. It's right at the train station/light rail and going through the train station gets you to the transit center.
Because it's south side of the street it's also a little closer to the 23/523 rapid line along San Carlos/ Stevens Creek though still a five to eight minute walk. This line really adds a lot of variety (ten minutes to Valley Fair, lots of other shopping all the way to Cupertino, and a second option to get downtown without walking). Whole Foods is across the street, and Ace Hardware a little bit down.
Cahill is a half mile closer to downtown, so you can easily walk from there (I do it almost daily). It's also an easier (though not pleasant because of going through construction and an industrial area) walk to Coleman shopping center which has everything you'll need for day to day. The walk up to the Alameda to catch the 22 or one of the other buses heading downtown is trivial but you have to go under the train tracks to get to a rapid stop.
Avalon is slightly better for access to The Alameda itself since it's more active on that block than the train area. It's a little more peaceful and an easier walk to the Rose Garden. The walking if you're a "go for a walk" kind of person is better on the western side. You can get to Coleman center via Taylor, but it's the far end (closer to Trader Joe's farther from Target). Avalon on the Alameda is right next to the rapid stop for the 22/522 so that is definitely a great option for getting many places, but you'll be more limited in destinations than from Cahill.
I would look at where you think you'll need to go over an average week, and if everything you need is walkable or you can get there on the 522, go with Alameda. Otherwise, Cahill.

Avalon is closer to bus transit and has some decent food choices. Cahill has a huge field that is great for dogs.
The area is generally safe, but you’ll see transient people all the time. Less so at Cahill.
If you work downtown I’d go with Avalon, if you need light rail then choose Cahill.
