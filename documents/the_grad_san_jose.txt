I know this building has had poor reviews in the past, but I do believe with the new management things have improved tremendously. Here are my top reasons for staying and enjoying it:
1. It’s a 5 minute walk straight to SJSU campus
2. Considering prices in the area, you get a lot for your money. Amenities, free WiFi, utilities included, and furniture included.
3. The building is beautiful with amazing views of the city.
4. The building is safe with overnight security and key fobs for entry doors.
5. Inside mail room and package lockers makes it easy and convenient for deliveries.
6. Maintenance is really good at following through.
7. You’re with kids your age, which is nice.
Something to consider:
The way leasing is structured makes it competitive once you’ve signed. They want you to sign early in exchange for same room guaranteed and the best price for the next year. However, it’s a huge commitment and who knows what you want to do in a year? Yet if you already sign you take yourself out of possibilities closer to when your lease ends. So you either sign early or wait with the risk of a larger rent increase or moving to another room… personally it’s a huge dilemma. There is a possibility of re-leasing your room to someone else, but I don’t believe it’s very successful.
This is the biggest thing I wish was different. It would be nice if they had traditional leasing with 30 days notice to either move or renew. Not trap you in a lease like 9-10 months ahead.
All together I think the trade-offs are well worth it.
